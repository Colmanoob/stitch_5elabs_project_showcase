5E Labs

5E Labs is a collection of virtual platforms for learning science and mathematics through immersive, gamified virtual labs. Built on the 5E inquiry-based learning framework, 5E Labs engage students in meaningful learning by guiding them through pedagogical stages for exploring abstract concepts in immersive virtual collaborative gaming environments.

About 5E Labs

Learning through immersive virtual inquiry

5E Labs showcases research-driven virtual learning platforms developed to support inquiry-based learning across mathematics, science, and teacher education.

The platforms are grounded in the 5E Inquiry Framework: Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, and Evaluate. Each environment gives learners space to investigate ideas, discuss evidence, test assumptions, and reflect on what they have learned.

Objectives

What the project is designed to support

Conceptual understanding

Help learners move from abstract concepts to visible, testable, and discussable learning experiences.

Inquiry practice

Guide students through questioning, exploration, explanation, application, and reflection.

Teacher transformation

Give educators a practical way to model inquiry-based pedagogy using digital learning spaces.

Impact

Virtual learning environments for science, mathematics, and teacher education

The 5E Labs collection connects curriculum goals with interactive learning experiences. It supports students who need concrete visual models and teachers who need safe spaces to practice inquiry-based facilitation.

The project emphasizes engagement, conceptual understanding, reflective learning, collaboration, and research-informed classroom practice.

5E Inquiry Framework

The learning cycle behind the platforms

EngageIntroduce a meaningful challenge or scenario.
ExploreLet learners investigate through interaction and evidence.
ExplainSupport learners as they describe patterns and concepts.
ElaborateExtend ideas into new situations and problems.
EvaluateEncourage reflection, feedback, and evidence of understanding.

21st Century Skills

Skills developed through the learning environments

Learners practice problem solving, collaboration, communication, digital literacy, evidence-based reasoning, and self-reflection while working through immersive scenarios.

The platforms are designed to connect disciplinary knowledge with practical decision-making in science, mathematics, and teaching contexts.

Philosophy behind 5E Labs

Learning by doing, explaining, and reflecting

5E Labs treats virtual environments as learning laboratories. Instead of only presenting content, each platform asks learners to act, test, discuss, and revise their understanding.

The philosophy is simple: students learn deeply when they can experience a concept, talk about it, connect it to evidence, and apply it beyond the first task.
