5E Labs / Mathematics

Math City 1.0

A classroom-ready virtual platform for learning properties of 3D shapes via collaborative inquiry.

Overview

Learning 3D shapes through a virtual city

Math City 1.0 places learners inside a virtual city where buildings, paths, and objects become concrete models for geometric reasoning.

Students investigate properties of 3D shapes through exploration, peer discussion, explanation, and application instead of only reading definitions.

Learning Design

Platform structure

3D Modeling

Geometric concepts are represented through visible 3D city objects that students can observe, compare, and discuss.

Gamification

The city setting gives learners goals and movement through the environment while keeping attention on mathematical thinking.

Design Thinking

Activities prompt students to connect shape properties with practical spatial problems in the virtual city.

Human-AI Interaction

The platform can support guided prompts, feedback, and learner reflection around the decisions students make while exploring.

21st Century Skills

Students practice collaboration, communication, digital literacy, evidence-based reasoning, and problem solving.
