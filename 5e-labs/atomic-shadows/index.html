Atomic Shadows

An immersive science learning environment for atomic structure, radiation, scientific ethics, and narrative-driven inquiry.

Overview

Story-based inquiry into atomic science

Atomic Shadows: Unstable Realms uses narrative and virtual exploration to help learners investigate abstract scientific ideas that are difficult to observe directly.

Students examine atomic phenomena, radiation concepts, and ethical questions through a learning environment built for evidence, discussion, and reflection.

Learning Design

Platform structure

Atomic Structure

Virtual representations help students reason about particles, structure, and scientific models.

Radiation Concepts

Learners investigate radiation-related ideas through contextualized scenarios rather than isolated facts.

Scientific Ethics

The platform connects science learning with responsible decision-making and societal consequences.

Narrative Inquiry

Story-driven tasks give learners a reason to question, gather evidence, and explain what they discover.

Reflective Practice

Students are encouraged to connect scientific evidence with personal reasoning and group discussion.
