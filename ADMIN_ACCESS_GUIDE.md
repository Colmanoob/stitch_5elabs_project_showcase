# Admin Access Guide: Registration & Login

This guide will walk you through accessing the administrative panel of the 5E Labs Project Showcase website. From the admin panel, you can manage users, upload files, and update project descriptions.

## 1. Accessing the Admin Page

To reach the administrative area, navigate to:
`https://5elabs.net/admin.php`

Alternatively, you can click the **Account** icon (circle icon) in the top-right corner of the homepage navigation bar.

## 3. Registering a New Account

If you wish to create a separate account for another team member, follow these steps:

1. On the Login page (`admin.php`), click the **"Register here"** link at the bottom.
2. Fill in the following details:
   - **Username**: A unique name for the new user.
   - **Email**: A valid email address (used for password recovery).
   - **Password**: Create a strong password.
3. Click **Register**.

### Approval Process:

To maintain security, new accounts are **not active immediately**.

- After registering, a **Superadmin** (like the `boss` or `Singh` account) must log in and navigate to the **User Management** section.
- The Superadmin must manually **Approve** the new account before it can be used to log in.

---

## 4. Password Recovery

If you forget your password:

1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login screen.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. You will receive a link to reset your password.

---

## 5. Security Best Practices

- **Change your initial password:** After logging in for the first time with the `Singh` account, it is recommended to change the password in the user profile settings.
- **Log Out:** Always click the **Logout** button when you are finished managing the site, especially on shared computers.
